## 2. Clinical practice

### Primary focus — Shoulder & Upper Limb
Dr Myhill's strongest clinical interest and primary marketing focus. Approximately one-third sports, one-third degenerative, one-third trauma.

**Key procedures:**
- Rotator cuff repair (arthroscopic and open)
- Shoulder replacement — anatomic total shoulder and reverse total shoulder arthroplasty
- Shoulder instability — arthroscopic Bankart repair and Latarjet procedure
- AC joint surgery (separation and arthritis)
- Biceps tendon surgery (tenodesis and tenotomy)
- Tennis elbow (lateral epicondylitis)
- OK (Outerbridge-Kashiwagi) procedure for elbow arthritis
- UCL reconstruction (medial elbow instability)
- Cubital tunnel syndrome (ulnar nerve decompression)
- Carpal tunnel syndrome (median nerve decompression)
- Wrist fractures — distal radius and scaphoid

**Marketing priority within upper limb:** rotator cuff pathology and shoulder replacement.

---

### Secondary focus — Hip & Knee
Actively marketed alongside upper limb.

**Key procedures:**
- Total hip replacement (arthroplasty)
- Total knee replacement (arthroplasty)
- ACL reconstruction
- Meniscal repair and partial meniscectomy
- Knee arthroscopy
- Hip fractures and lower limb trauma

---

### Supporting area — Trauma
Upper and lower limb trauma accepted from GPs, physiotherapists and emergency departments. Long-term follow-up provided. Not the primary marketing lead.

## 3. Differentiator

Dr Myhill's stated point of difference is **shared decision-making**: a structured process in which patients actively participate in choosing their treatment or management plan, with choices fitted to their individual values, goals and lifestyle.

This is not a marketing claim — it is a clinical framework he practices deliberately and has trained in.

> *"Patients are collaborators, not passive recipients. The best plan is the one that fits your life."*

## 8. Compliance constraints

The following are non-negotiable requirements under the AHPRA Advertising Guidelines and the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth):

- **No patient testimonials** anywhere — website, social, print
- **No comparative claims** — "best", "#1", "leading", "top-rated"
- **No guaranteed outcomes** — "pain-free", "full recovery guaranteed"
- **No unsubstantiated statistics** — success rates, patient numbers, etc.
- **No inducements** — discounts, urgency tactics
- **Credentials must be accurate** — MBBS · FRACS(Ortho) · MPH · BSc only; fellowship described as "IBRA Scholarship at Erasmus MC, Rotterdam"
- **Privacy Policy** compliant with Australian Privacy Principles
- Site must be reviewed by indemnity insurer or health law adviser before publicising
