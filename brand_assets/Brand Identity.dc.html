01 · The Mark
The patient at the centre.
Movement restored around them.
An open ring traces the shoulder’s arc of motion, the discipline at the heart of the practice. Its opening signals a system that invites the patient in rather than closing around them — shared decision-making, made visual. The fixed central point is the person at the centre of every plan.

04 · Brand Pillars
Four ideas every word, image and decision should answer to.
01
The patient at the centre
Shared decision-making is the practice, not a slogan. Plans are built around a person’s values, goals and lifestyle, chosen with them, never handed down.
02
Movement restored
A sub-specialist focus on the shoulder and upper limb. Surgery is never the point in itself. It is in service of getting a life back in motion.
03
Measured expertise
Fellowship-trained and evidence-led. The tone is calm and rigorous: confident without ever being promotional, comparative or absolute.
04
Plainly spoken
Fast, clear correspondence for referrers; plain-English guidance for patients. Australian, considered, and never reliant on jargon left unexplained.
