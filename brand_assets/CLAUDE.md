# Project rules

## Copywriting
- **Never use em dashes** (— or `&mdash;`) in any deliverable. Use a comma, colon, or full stop within sentences, and a middot (`&middot;`) for label/eyebrow separators.

## Brand positioning — Dr Nate Myhill
- The patient always has agency and always participates in the decision. Shared decision-making is the spine of the brand: the plan is built *with* the patient, never *for* them. Copy should keep the patient an active participant in every choice, never a passive recipient.
- **Generalist + specialist, in that order (wide front door, deep back room).** Lead with breadth so a GP never has to think about scope: a general orthopaedic surgeon who takes the whole limb, shoulder to knee, sports to trauma. Then signal depth: a *special interest* in the shoulder, upper limb and sports. Never brand as shoulder-only (it loses the wrist/knee/general referrals, then the shoulders too). Use the phrase "special interest in…" (accurate and AHPRA-safe), not a sub-specialty title claim.
